    Limitations

    1. Per 100 g, as listed

      Values are for 100 g of the food, mostly raw or as sold. Cooking changes them.

    2. Fibre differs by database

      India's IFCT often reports about 2 times the fibre of USDA and CoFID for the same food, such as guava or garlic, because of method and which parts are measured.

    3. 121 foods have no sugar value

      The source never measured it. We did not fill it in; scores count it as 0 and it is marked.

    4. 12 source errors corrected

      For example, the digital IFCT copy lists oils and ghee at 0 kcal. Energy was calculated from fat at 9 kcal per g, and every fix is noted.

    5. The score will keep evolving

      Some results may still look inconsistent. We are working through the more complex factors behind healthy ageing, and which biomarkers and nutrients matter most, so the scoring model will keep improving.
